JanSetu AI 🇮🇳

Connecting Citizens to Government Support

JanSetu AI is a smart welfare assistance platform designed to help citizens easily discover government schemes, check eligibility, avoid fake schemes, and access official government resources in one place.

The platform especially focuses on helping rural citizens, first-time applicants, and people who struggle to navigate multiple government websites and complex eligibility systems.

🚨 Problem Statement

The Government releases many welfare schemes for citizens, but a large number of people never benefit from them because:

Information is spread across multiple central and state government portals
Citizens do not know which schemes they are eligible for
Rural and less educated users struggle to understand eligibility criteria
Many people depend on local middlemen or unofficial sources
Fake scheme scams and fraudulent websites mislead users

JanSetu AI solves this problem by creating a centralized, user-friendly, and AI-powered welfare discovery platform.
